FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Skill Intelligence.

Get Started Free →
How does the AI skill assessment work?
−
Our AI-proctored assessments test domain knowledge, applied reasoning, and hands-on proficiency across your selected skill tracks. Results are benchmarked against 4,200+ enterprise engineers nationwide to give you a verified competency score and peer percentile ranking.
What is the AI Career Diagnostics panel?
+
The AI Career Diagnostics panel runs a neural evaluation of your verified skills against your target role requirements. It produces an overall readiness score (0–100%), identifies your top strengths and high-priority gaps, and estimates time-to-readiness.
Are certifications industry-recognized?
+
Yes. Our certifications are recognized by our network of 50+ enterprise partners across India's top AI and tech companies. Each certificate includes a unique verification ID and shareable URL.
Is Skill Intelligence free to use?
+
Core features including profile creation, basic skill assessments, and community access are free. Advanced features like AI Career Diagnostics, full benchmark analytics, personalized AI learning paths, and dossier export are available under enterprise and professional plans.
How does the leaderboard ranking work?
+
Your rank is computed from weighted assessment scores, Karma XP (earned through endorsements, streak, and community activity), and verified competency breadth. Rankings are refreshed weekly and segmented nationally and by specialization track.
Can enterprises use Skill Intelligence to hire?
+
Absolutely. Enterprises access a verified talent pool filtered by benchmark score, role-readiness percentage, specialization (LLMs, MLOps, Vector DBs, etc.), and location. Our enterprise dashboard provides direct candidate matching and outreach tools.
